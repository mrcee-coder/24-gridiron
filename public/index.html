03 • Side games

Easy to play.
Hard to blame the commissioner.

Both games get a visible leaderboard on this site. Entries lock automatically and scoring should be automatic wherever the league data makes that possible.

Weekly game 01

WHO SAID IT?

One anonymous answer from the manager questionnaire appears each week.

  1. Every manager gets one guess at who said it.
  2. You cannot guess yourself.
  3. Guesses lock at the first NFL kickoff of the week.
  4. Correct guess = 1 point.
  5. The quote's owner is revealed after the scoring period ends.
  6. Each submitted profile answer is used at most once until the pool resets.

Tracking: the site stores guesses, locks them, reveals the answer and maintains the season standings. Your job is basically choosing/rejecting a quote if the automatic selection is bad.

Season leaderboardHighest total points wins • prize determined by ballot
Weekly game 02

GRIDIRON FORECAST

Predict three things about our league before the week starts — not NFL betting lines.

  1. Pick the week's highest-scoring team.
  2. Pick the biggest standings upset: the lower-ranked team beating the opponent furthest above them.
  3. Pick the closest matchup by final margin.
  4. Each correct category = 1 point; maximum 3 per week.
  5. Picks lock at the first NFL kickoff of the week.
  6. Ties in a category count as correct for every matching selection.

Tracking: Sleeper results can score all three categories automatically and update the leaderboard after the week finalizes. No manual grading.

05 • Constitution & rulings

The rulebook should make arguments boring.

When the founding ballot closes, each winning option becomes an official rule with its vote result and effective date. Future competitive changes happen in the offseason under the voting threshold the league chooses. During the season, the commissioner can enforce written rules and correct platform or clerical errors — not invent a new rule because a result feels unfair.
