SIDE GAMES

Two tiny games layered on top of the real league. The site handles the records, locks and standings so nobody has to keep a spreadsheet.

Checking season state…
Game 01

Who Said It?

A real answer from one manager's preseason profile is posted anonymously. Guess who wrote it.

Preseason setup

The quote pool fills as managers submit their founding ballot profiles. Weekly rounds begin with the regular season.

  1. One anonymous quote each week.
  2. One guess per manager; you cannot guess yourself.
  3. Guesses lock at the first NFL kickoff.
  4. Correct guess = 1 point.
  5. Answer is revealed after the scoring period.
  6. No quote is reused until the available pool cycles.

Automation: profile answers supply the quote pool; submissions, locks, reveals and points live in the league database.

Game 02

Gridiron Forecast

Predict our league—not NFL lines—before the week begins.

Waiting for rosters

Once Sleeper is connected and the schedule exists, the weekly team/matchup choices appear here automatically.

  1. Pick the week's highest-scoring team.
  2. Pick the biggest standings upset.
  3. Pick the closest final matchup.
  4. Each correct category = 1 point; max 3.
  5. Entries lock at first NFL kickoff.
  6. Tied correct outcomes award the point to every matching pick.

Automation: Sleeper matchup results grade the three categories and feed the season table.
