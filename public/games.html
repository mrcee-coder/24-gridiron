Game 01 • Weekly

Who Said It?

A real answer from one league member's preseason profile is posted anonymously. Guess who wrote it.

Preseason setup

The quote pool fills as league members submit their founding profiles. Weekly rounds begin with the regular season.

  1. One anonymous quote each week.
  2. One guess per person; you cannot guess yourself.
  3. Guesses lock at the first NFL kickoff.
  4. Correct guess = 1 point.
  5. The answer is revealed after the scoring period.
  6. No quote is reused until the available pool cycles.

Automation: profile answers supply the quote pool; submissions, locks, reveals and points live in the league database.

Gridiron Gauntlet

Top-down endless football. Find the lane, dodge defenders and keep running until somebody puts you on the turf.

Farthest run wins.

Speed increases with distance. Your best verified yardage is saved automatically.

  2. Drag on your phone or use A/D / arrow keys.
  3. Touch a defender and the run ends.
  4. No daily limit. Keep trying.
  5. Only your best verified run counts.
  6. Season leaderboard updates automatically.
