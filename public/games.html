Game 01 • Weekly • Opens Week 1

Read the Room

Three quick questions. Your job is not to answer what you believe. Your job is to predict what answer the most other league members will choose.

THE ONE RULE THAT MATTERS

Do not choose your favorite answer. Choose the answer you think the most other people in this league will choose.

  1. Three multiple-choice questions each week.
  2. Predict the answer you think will receive the most league picks.
  3. Match the most-picked answer = 1 point.
  4. Three correct predictions = a 3-point perfect week.
  5. If two answers tie for most picks, either tied answer scores.
WHY IT'S WONKY

This is not a survey of what the league actually prefers. Everybody is sharing an assumption about everybody else. The winner means most predicted answer, not the league's true favorite.

And that is exactly why we're doing it. Because why not? By the end of the season, we'll know an absurd amount about what our colleagues assume the rest of us assume. And you know what happens when you assume.
