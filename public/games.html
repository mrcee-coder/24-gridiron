Game 01 • Weekly • 3 points available

Read the Room

Three quick questions. Your job is not to answer what you believe. Your job is to predict what answer the most other league members will choose.

THE ONE RULE THAT MATTERS

Do not choose your favorite answer. Choose the answer you think the most other people in this league will choose.

  1. Three multiple-choice questions are posted each week.
  2. For each question, predict the answer you think will receive the most league picks.
  3. If your prediction matches the most-picked answer, you score 1 point.
  4. Three correct predictions = a 3-point perfect week.
  5. If two answers tie for most picks, either tied answer scores the point.
  6. Entries lock before the weekly deadline and cannot be changed afterward.
EXAMPLE

Question: Best game-day food? Wings / Pizza / Tacos / Burgers.

Your actual favorite might be Tacos. But if you think most people here will choose Wings, your game answer is Wings.

WHY THE RESULTS ARE A LITTLE WONKY

This is not a survey of what the league actually prefers. Everybody is sharing an assumption about everybody else. So when the results reveal, the winner means “most predicted answer” — not “the league's true favorite.” If everyone thinks everyone else will pick wings, wings can win even if half the room personally prefers tacos.

Scoring: 1 point per correct room prediction, maximum 3 per week. The season table also tracks perfect weeks, prediction percentage and longest correct streak for bragging rights, but those do not add bonus points.
