THE ONE RULE THAT MATTERS

Do not choose your favorite answer. Choose the answer you think the most other people in this league will choose.

  1. Three multiple-choice questions each week.
  2. Predict the answer you think will receive the most league picks.
  3. Match the most-picked answer = 1 point.
  4. Three correct predictions = a 3-point perfect week.
  5. If two answers tie for most picks, either tied answer scores.
