Why bother?

Because eventually this becomes the good part.

Sleeper is excellent at telling us what is happening now. This is where we remember what happened. A random Week 4 loss in 2026 becomes funny when somebody is still complaining about it in 2030. The more seasons we play, the more valuable the archive gets.

Right now most of these records are empty. That is the point. Every season somebody gets to put their name on something that stays here.
